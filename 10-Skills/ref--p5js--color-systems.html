Color Systems

Color Modes

HSB (Recommended for Generative Art)

HSB advantages:

  • Rotate hue: (baseHue + offset) % 360
  • Desaturate: reduce S
  • Darken: reduce B
  • Monochrome variations: fix H, vary S and B
  • Complementary: (hue + 180) % 360
  • Analogous: hue +/- 30
